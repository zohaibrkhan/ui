# Example

## What it is

Two or three sentences: the job this section does on a page, who it suits, and the one idea that carries it.

## Layout anatomy

The structure top to bottom and left to right: grid, columns, what is pinned, what overlaps, how it reflows at tablet and mobile widths.

## Visual system

- **Type:** families observed, roles (display / body / mono), scale contrast.
- **Colour:** the palette as roles (background, ink, accent) with the observed values.
- **Shape and depth:** radii, borders, shadows, texture.

## Motion and interaction

What moves, what triggers it (load, scroll, hover, pointer, input), and how it degrades with reduced motion.

## Techniques worth reusing

- **Technique name** - how it works and when to reach for it. Link to `../../../patterns/<slug>.md` when a shared pattern covers it.

## Build brief

An original prompt, written in our own words, for building a *new* section in this pattern. It describes structure, behaviour and quality bars generically and uses placeholder content. It is not the vendor's prompt and must not reproduce their copy, brand names or artwork.

```text

```

Where it fits, what to change first, accessibility and performance cautions.
